Our Story

Brewed with care, served with heart

1Shot! began with a simple belief: the best moments of the day can fit inside a single shot of espresso. We source specialty beans, roast them in small batches, and pull every shot by hand so your cup tastes the same on a busy Monday as it does on a slow Sunday.

Beyond coffee, we serve creamy milk teas and a counter full of pastries — flaky croissants, fudgy brownies, and cupcakes baked in-house each morning. Come for the caffeine, stay for the warm seats and good company.
